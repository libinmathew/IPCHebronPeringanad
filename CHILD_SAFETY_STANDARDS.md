# Child Safety Standards & CSAE Policy
**IPC Hebron Peringanad**

**Last Updated: August 22, 2026**

IPC Hebron Peringanad is committed to providing a safe and secure environment for all members of our community, especially children. We have a zero-tolerance policy for any form of child sexual abuse, exploitation, or the sharing of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

## 1. Zero-Tolerance Policy
We strictly prohibit the use of our platform for:
*   The solicitation, grooming, or exploitation of minors.
*   The upload, storage, or distribution of CSAM (Child Sexual Abuse Material).
*   Any behavior that endangers the physical or emotional well-being of a child.

## 2. Safety Standards & CSAE Prevention
To comply with global safety standards and Google Play’s Child Safety policy:
*   **Access Control**: Our app is a closed community directory. Access is restricted to verified church members.
*   **Privacy by Design**: We provide granular privacy controls (e.g., the "Show Phone" toggle) to allow users to control their visibility.
*   **Prohibited Content**: Users are strictly forbidden from uploading any content that depicts or encourages the exploitation of children.

## 3. Reporting Mechanisms
We encourage all users to report any suspicious activity or potential policy violations immediately.
*   **In-App Reporting**: Users can report profiles or content through the contact information provided in the "Contact Us" section of the app.
*   **Direct Email**: Report any concerns regarding child safety directly to: **[Insert Church Safety Email]**.
*   **Urgent Concerns**: If a child is in immediate danger, please contact local law enforcement authorities immediately.

## 4. Content Moderation & Response
*   **Investigation**: All reports of child safety violations are investigated immediately by our administrative team.
*   **Action**: Accounts found to be in violation of this policy will be permanently banned and data will be preserved for law enforcement.
*   **Reporting to Authorities**: We comply with legal requirements to report suspected child exploitation and CSAM to the relevant authorities, such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or local police.

## 5. Law Enforcement Cooperation
IPC Hebron Peringanad cooperates fully with law enforcement agencies in the investigation of crimes against children. We will disclose user information when required by legal process or when we have a good faith belief that such disclosure is necessary to protect a child.
